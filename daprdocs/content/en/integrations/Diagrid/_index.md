[Diagrid](https://www.diagrid.io/) is a leading contributor to the Dapr project and offers a range of tooling and commercial products that build on open source Dapr. The pages in this section describe the Diagrid offerings you can use alongside your Dapr applications:

- [**Diagrid Catalyst**]({{< ref catalyst.md >}}) — durable agentic execution for AI agents, built on Dapr.
- [**Dapr Enterprise Support**]({{< ref dapr-support.md >}}) — commercial support for open source Dapr.
- [**Dapr Ops Dashboard**]({{< ref dapr-ops-dashboard.md >}}) — free platform for automated operations and observability for Dapr on Kubernetes.
- [**Dapr Dev Dashboard**]({{< ref dev-dashboard.md >}}) — a free companion for local Dapr development.
- [**D3E**]({{< ref d3e.md >}}) — a custom distribution of open source Dapr with additional security and scalability capabilities.
